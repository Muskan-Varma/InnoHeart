# 🌟 SARAS - Your Creative Marketplace 🎨  

Welcome to **SARAS**, a platform crafted for creators and artists to showcase and sell their unique handmade crafts. Whether you're a professional artisan or a hobbyist, SARAS is your go-to space for turning passion into profit. 🚀  

---  

## 🖌️ **About SARAS**  
SARAS is not just a marketplace; it's a community. Our mission is to empower every creator by eliminating the need for business tags, professional profiles, or complex processes. SARAS focuses on simplicity, transparency, and creativity, much like OLX but exclusively for handmade and artistic creations.  

---  

## ✨ **Features**  
- **Dynamic Craft Cards**: Interactive cards showcasing crafts with images, details, and seamless navigation.  
- **Favorites List**: Save crafts you love and access them anytime.  
- **Direct Buyer-Seller Contact**: Transparent communication for smooth transactions.  
- **Secure Platform**: Safe space for creators to sell without intermediaries.  

---  

## ⚙️ **Technologies Used**  
- **Frontend**: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Bootstrap  
- **Backend**: Core PHP  
- **Database**: MySQL  
- **Version Control**: Git & GitHub
